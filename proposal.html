A PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT CONCEPT

Improving the vehicle discovery and dealer experience

An independent proposal informed by a review of publicly accessible pages and screenshots of the Cheki Motors website.

Important: This is an unofficial concept. It is not endorsed by Cheki Motors, does not use its private systems and does not claim that the proposed features are absent from the live platform.

What we observed

  • The vehicle category page presents separate Advanced Search, Keyword Search and Filter Search entry points.
  • The listing cards show vehicle photos, asking prices, titles, location and some seller indicators.
  • The vehicle detail page includes a gallery area, specifications, location, features, description and dealer information.
  • The dealer profile provides Inventory, About and Contact tabs.
  • The supplied About screenshot showed a large blank content region; this may be empty profile content or a loading/rendering issue and requires confirmation.
  • The supplied Contact screenshot showed a map area with the text “false”; the underlying location/configuration should be investigated.

Proposed areas to explore

  1. Vehicle discovery: make search and filters easier to find and understand; improve result sorting and no-result guidance.
  2. Listing quality: present key facts consistently and make sponsored placements clear.
  3. Vehicle details: improve gallery navigation and structure specifications and seller descriptions.
  4. Dealer profiles: improve completeness, contact clarity and inventory presentation.
  5. Trust and safety: explain seller/listing status precisely, provide reporting and encourage independent inspections. Do not imply inspection or verification unless it has actually occurred.
  6. Mobile experience: test search, listing cards and dealer pages at common phone widths.

Suggested delivery approach

01 · Discovery

Validate observations with the Cheki team and agree on measurable priorities.

02 · UX prototype

Review a clickable concept for discovery, vehicle details and dealer profiles.

03 · Technical assessment

With authorization, review the existing stack, accessibility, performance and integration constraints.

04 · Implementation plan

Agree scope, ownership, milestones, testing and rollout before any production changes.
